Makers Department Founder. Grew the team from one to five and ran three to four projects at once. 2015 – now

I started Makers Department in 2015 and incorporated it in 2016. My first contractor came on in 2018 and my first full-time employee in 2019. We were mostly industrial designers until 2022, when a senior design engineer joined to own design for manufacture across our projects. At our peak, in 2021 and 2022, we were four to five people running three to four projects at the same time.

How I ran it. Short stand-ups where everyone shared what they'd done and got feedback from the others, planning at the start of each week, and every project tracked against its budget and timeline. I took on the stakeholder problems so the team could keep working.

How I trained and motivated people. One-to-one sessions when I spotted a skill gap, such as CAD surfacing. Social Fridays visiting local makers, workshops and creative people. And time set aside for our own projects, with looser briefs than client work allows, so everyone had something they owned.

UNIT9 Industrial designer and head of fabrication inside an agency team of five, for Škoda. 2026

I joined UNIT9's team for the Škoda DuoBell, working alongside their design engineer and a CAD designer, with a workshop on manufacture. I managed the mechanical design, brought in the University of Salford's research and took the bell through suppliers into production.

Read the DuoBell case study

Hyphen Design Started as a junior designer, grew into running the McLaren programme. 2013 – 2024

Client work for Bosch Appliances, McLaren and Tangle Teezer, among others. I moved from sketches and CAD into project and operations management, and two of the accounts, McLaren and Dishmatic, carried on with me when I set up my own studio.

Read the McLaren case study

Hammond Studios with KMI Lead industrial designer on Ted Baker's 88 fragrance bottles, launched worldwide in 2019. 2017 – 2018

Hammond Studios partnered with the agency KMI on a fragrance line for Ted Baker. I was the main industrial designer, designing and engineering a series of perfume bottles in several sizes. The range was manufactured and launched worldwide in 2019.

Fuse Prototype manager between their creative team and my technical team. 2022

Fuse was developing an interactive wand: an electromechanical device full of motion sensors that reacts differently to different gestures. I managed the prototype, translating between their creative team and my technical team, and took it from rough concepts to a working prototype for demonstrations.

Kingston University Taught project management to creative students working on live briefs from the BBC and Gov.uk. 2023

Part-time tutor in the Creative Industries department, teaching project management to first- and second-year media production and marketing students: 30 to 35 at a time, working in teams of five. We brought in live briefs from the BBC, Gov.uk and other organisations, and students presented their work to people who actually work there. Some projects went on to become real, and some students got placements from them.

How I taught. With a co-teacher, we opened each phase with examples and case studies, then set exercises so students applied the methods to their own projects. My part was going table by table: giving feedback, and showing teams practical ways to brainstorm, run a productive discussion and gather feedback.

PurePods Designer and shareholder. Design, manufacturing, Amazon, stock and logistics. 2020 – 2025

PurePods started as a design job and became a business I part-owned. I designed the refillable filter and developed it with our overseas manufacturers, then ended up running most of the rest too: pricing, the Amazon and Shopify stores, marketing, and logistics across the UK and EU.

When Brita changed their jugs after our launch and broke compatibility, we designed an adapter, 3D-printed and checked for safety and patents first, then tooled for volume. We sent it free to customers who complained and later shipped it with every pack. Having my own money in a product taught me to think about cost and margin, not just form.
